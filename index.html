CHAPTER ONE

The Story Behind Her Name

Every name has a story. Ours began long before she arrived.

Before I arrived, Mom and Dad searched for a name filled with love and grace. Their journey began with countless conversations, endless lists, and quiet evenings imagining the little girl they were about to meet. Wanting my name to forever connect them both, they chose the letter K—the first letter they shared—as the beginning of my story. After days of searching, just one day before I was born, they found the name Kaleigh. The moment they said it aloud... they knew. It simply felt like me.

Franzelle

A name made with love.

To complete my name, Mom and Dad looked to Dad's second name, Franz. With a gentle touch, they created Franzelle— a beautiful bridge connecting father and daughter forever. Together, Kaleigh Franzelle Cordova Occo became more than a name. It became the first gift my parents ever gave me. A love letter that I'll carry for the rest of my life.
