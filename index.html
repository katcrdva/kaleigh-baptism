CHAPTER ONE

The Story Behind Her Name

Every name has a story.
Ours began long before she arrived.

Before she arrived, we searched for a name filled with love and grace.

Our journey began with countless conversations, endless lists, and quiet evenings imagining the little girl we were about to meet.

Wanting her name to forever connect us both, we chose the letter K— the first letter we shared—as the beginning of her story.

After days of searching, just one day before she was born, we found the name Kaleigh.

The moment we said it aloud...
we knew.
It simply felt like her.

Franzelle

A name made with love.

To complete her name, we looked to Dad's second name, Franz.

With a gentle touch, we created Franzelle— a beautiful bridge connecting father and daughter forever.

Together, Kaleigh Franzelle Cordova Occo became more than a name.

It became the first gift we ever gave her.

A love letter that she'll carry for the rest of her life.
